Coverfold: CD, DVD and Blu-ray covers to print at home

Coverfold is a free web app to design packaging for your discs and print it at home on A4. Drop your images on the panels, add your text, and export a PDF that prints at exact size, with bleed, crop marks, fold lines and assembly instructions.

What you can make

  • Jewel case booklets (single leaf, 4 pages or saddle-stitched), tray cards and obi strips
  • DVD and Blu-ray covers, standard and slim, and slipcovers
  • Cardboard sleeves, sleeves with a flap, paper inner sleeves and slipcases
  • Digipaks and CD wallets, with a 3D-printable disc tray made to measure
  • Disc labels on A4 sticker paper

How it works

  1. Pick the packaging. Every dieline is generated from measurements, so you can match a real case.
  2. Drop your images and add text, tracklists and QR codes. Bleed is added to every panel for you.
  3. Export the PDF and print it on A4. Pieces larger than A4 are split along a fold, with glue tabs.

Features

  • No account and no upload: your pictures stay on your computer.
  • Works offline after the first visit, and installs as an app.
  • 3D preview that folds the printed sheet into the finished case.
  • AI upscaling for small pictures, on your own graphics card.
  • Album art and tracklists from MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive.
  • English and Italian.

Questions

Is Coverfold free?

Yes. There is nothing to pay, no account and no ads. The source code is under the PolyForm Noncommercial license.

What paper should I use?

120 to 170 g/m² paper for booklets and tray cards, 250 to 300 g/m² cardstock for sleeves, digipaks and slipcases, and A4 sticker paper for disc labels.

Will it print at the right size?

Yes. The PDF is at real scale on A4 and can include a 100 mm ruler, so you can check that the printer did not resize it.

Where are my projects saved?

In your browser, on your computer. You can export a backup file at any time.
